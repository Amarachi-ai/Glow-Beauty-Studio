About Glow Beauty Studio

Beauty, confidence and individuality are at the heart of everything we do.

Our Story

Glow Beauty Studio was established in 2023 in Johannesburg, South Africa, after its founder noticed a growing demand for affordable and personalised beauty services.

The business started as a small home-based beauty service and grew through word-of-mouth recommendations. It eventually developed into a professional beauty studio offering makeup, eyelashes, nails and skincare services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality beauty services in an inclusive and welcoming environment where clients of all races, cultures and backgrounds feel valued, confident and beautiful.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and inclusive beauty studio in Johannesburg, recognised for providing high-quality and personalised beauty services while celebrating diversity and helping every client feel confident, valued and beautiful.

Beauty For Everyone

At Glow Beauty Studio, we believe that beauty has no single definition. We welcome clients from all races, cultures and backgrounds and aim to create an environment where everyone feels comfortable and respected.
