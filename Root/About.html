History

Glow Beauty Studio was established in 2023 in Johannesburg, South Africa, after its founder noticed a growing demand for affordable, personalised beauty service
The business started as a small home-based beauty service and grew through word of mouth recommendations, eventually developing into a professional beauty studio offering makeup, eyelashes, nails and skincare services

Our Mission is to provide quality beauty services in an inclusive and welcoming environment where clients of all races, cultures and backgrounds feel valued, cofident and beautiful

Our Vision is to become a trusted and inclusive beauty studio in Johannesburg, recognised for providing highquality and personalised beauty service while celebrating diversity and making clientrs of all races, cultures and backgrounds feel confident, valued and beautiful
